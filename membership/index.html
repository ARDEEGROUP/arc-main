How it works

Pay once. Get more to spend.

Every membership turns into Credits. 1 Credit = ₹100 at the club, and every plan gives you more Credits than you pay for.

Step 1

Choose your membership

Prime, Signature or Reserve, for 6 or 12 months. Prime also runs month to month.

Step 2

Your Credits load

Pay in full and every Credit is added the day you join. Pay a 12-month plan in 2 instalments and half load at each. Prime Monthly loads month by month.

Step 3

Spend anywhere at ARC

Any court, The Club Table, coaching, events and the pro shop, at ARC Gurugram, New Friends Colony and Goa. Shared with family on your plan.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How do members book?

Directly with us by call or WhatsApp, at the front desk, or in the ARC app once it launches. Members book ahead of pay and play: up to 7 days on Prime Monthly, 10 on Prime, 12 on Signature and 14 on Reserve, against 3 days for pay and play.

What if I need to cancel a booking?

Cancel free up to 12 hours before, and any time if rain stops play on an outdoor court. Your first late cancellation in a term is a friendly reminder. After that, the Credits for the booking are used.

How long do Credits last?

For your plan's term: 6 or 12 months, or 30 days on Prime Monthly. On 6- and 12-month plans you can extend by 3 months for ₹5,000 plus GST, once a term. You can also pause free for up to 30 days in a term with a doctor's note. Credits have no cash value.

Can my family use my membership?

Add a partner for 50% of your plan price or a junior aged 5–17 for 20%, plus GST. Each adds the same share of your plan's Credits to one shared balance. Plan privileges stay with the main member. Guests can play when the main member is present.

Is the gym included?

The gym and group classes are run by our partners at Nine Wellness and added to any membership as Wellness by Nine: ₹40,000 or 400 Credits for 12 months, or ₹26,000 or 260 Credits for 6 months, per person. Pilates is separate and paid in Credits.

Who can join?

Members must be 18 or over. Juniors aged 5–17 join as family members on a parent's plan. Prime is limited to 40, Signature to 25, Reserve to 10 and Prime Monthly to 100. When a tier is full, new applicants join a waiting list.

Can I pay in instalments?

12-month plans can be paid in full, with every Credit loading the day you join, or in 2 equal instalments 6 months apart, with half the Credits loading at each. 6-month plans are paid in full. Prime Monthly is paid upfront each month.

Can I transfer or cancel a membership?

6- and 12-month memberships can be transferred to someone else once a term for ₹5,000 plus GST, and your remaining Credits move with it. Prime Monthly can be cancelled any time before the next payment. Memberships are non-refundable.
